## Customization Guidelines

### Brand Voice
- Professional but approachable
- Clear and direct (no jargon)
- Confident but humble
- Customer-focused ("you" not "we")

### Tone Adjustments by Channel

**Website/Landing Page:**
- Energetic and inspiring
- Focus on transformation
- Use social proof heavily

**Email:**
- Conversational and helpful
- Personal (from a real person)
- Action-oriented with clear CTAs

**Demo/Sales:**
- Consultative and educational
- Listen more than talk
- Focus on their specific problems

**Support/Success:**
- Patient and empathetic
- Proactive and anticipatory
- Celebrate wins

## Metrics to Track

### Website
- Visitors → Trial signups (goal: 5%)
- Time on page (goal: 2+ minutes)
- Scroll depth (goal: 75%+)
- CTA click rate (goal: 10%+)

### Email
- Open rate (goal: 25%+)
- Click rate (goal: 5%+)
- Trial activation (goal: 60%+)
- Trial → Paid (goal: 20%+)

### Demo
- Show rate (goal: 80%+)
- Demo → Trial (goal: 60%+)
- Demo satisfaction (goal: 4.5/5)

### Content
- Organic traffic growth (goal: 20% MoM)
- Keyword rankings (goal: top 10 for 5+ terms)
- Backlinks (goal: 50+ in first year)

## A/B Testing Ideas

### Landing Page
- Hero headline variations
- CTA button copy ("Start Free Trial" vs "Get Started Free")
- Pricing display (monthly vs annual default)
- Social proof placement

### Email
- Subject line styles (question vs statement)
- Send time (morning vs afternoon)
- Email length (short vs detailed)
- CTA placement (top vs bottom)

### Demo
- Demo length (10 min vs 15 min)
- Question order (problem-first vs solution-first)
- Pricing timing (early vs late in demo)

## Localization Notes

For international expansion, prioritize:

1. **UK/Europe:**
   - Adjust pricing to GBP/EUR
   - GDPR compliance messaging
   - "Whilst" vs "while", "organisation" vs "organization"

2. **Australia:**
   - Similar to US/UK
   - Emphasize data residency options

3. **Asia-Pacific:**
   - More formal tone
   - Emphasize security and compliance
   - Longer sales cycles expected

# BaceVector Demo Script

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Goal:** Show value, address objections, schedule trial

---

## Pre-Demo Preparation

### Before the Call
- [ ] Review prospect's company website and LinkedIn

## Problem Validation (2 minutes)

### Show Current State

"Let me show you what most teams do today when they need to find information..."

**Screen share: Typical document folder structure**
- Open SharePoint/Google Drive with nested folders
- Show 50+ documents with similar names
- Demonstrate keyword search returning 100+ results
- "Which one has the current policy? How do you know?"

**Key Point:** "Your team probably spends 10-15 minutes per search, multiple times per day. That adds up to 2+ hours daily per employee."

---

## Solution Introduction (1 minute)

"BaceVector changes this completely. Instead of searching through folders, your team asks questions naturally, like they would ask a colleague."

**Transition:** "Let me show you how it works with documents similar to yours..."

---

## Live Demo (8 minutes)

### Part 1: Document Upload (2 minutes)

**Action:** Upload a sample document (e.g., Employee Handbook for HR prospect)

"First, you upload your documents. We support PDFs, Word docs, and text files. You can upload one at a time or drag in a whole folder."

**Show:**
- Drag and drop interface
- Upload progress bar
- Document processing status
- Metadata fields (optional tags, categories)

**Key Point:** "The AI reads and understands your document in about 30 seconds. For a 50-page handbook, that's faster than you could even open it."

### Part 2: Natural Language Queries (4 minutes)

**Action:** Ask 3-4 questions relevant to their use case

**Example for HR:**

**Query 1:** "What is our vacation policy?"

**Show:**
- Type question in search box
- Instant response (< 2 seconds)
- Answer with specific details
- Source citation with document name and section
- "View source" button that highlights exact location

**Key Point:** "Notice how it doesn't just find the document—it gives you the exact answer with the source. Your employee can verify it instantly."

---

**Query 2:** "How do I submit an expense report?"

**Show:**
- Step-by-step answer
- Multiple source citations (if procedure spans documents)
- Related questions suggested at bottom

**Key Point:** "It understands context. This is a 'how-to' question, so it gives you the steps in order."

---

**Query 3:** "Can I work remotely from another country?"

**Show:**
- Nuanced answer with conditions
- Citations from multiple policy sections
- Confidence indicator (if applicable)

**Key Point:** "This is a complex question with conditions. BaceVector pulls from multiple sections to give a complete answer."

---

**Query 4:** (Custom to their industry/role)

**For HR:** "What benefits are available to part-time employees?"
**For IT:** "What's the process for requesting server access?"
**For Sales:** "What's our pricing for enterprise customers?"

**Pause for questions:** "What questions would your team typically ask?"

**Take 1-2 questions from prospect and answer live if possible**

### Part 3: Admin Dashboard (2 minutes)

**Action:** Show analytics and management features

**Show:**
- Usage dashboard (queries per day, top questions)
- Document management (add, update, delete)
- User management (invite team, set permissions)

**Key Points:**
- "See what your team is asking—this helps you identify knowledge gaps"
- "Update a document anytime, changes are instant"
- "You'll get alerts at 80% and 100% of your query limit, no surprises"

## Objection Handling (1 minute)

### Common Objections & Responses

**"How accurate is it?"**
"Great question. The AI is trained on your specific documents, and every answer includes source citations so your team can verify. In our testing, accuracy is 95%+, and you can always update documents if something changes."

**"What about security?"**
"Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We're SOC 2 compliant, and your documents are never used to train AI models. You control who has access with role-based permissions."

**"We already use [SharePoint/Confluence/etc.]"**
"Perfect! BaceVector works alongside those tools. Think of it as a smart search layer on top of your existing documents. You don't need to change your workflow."

**"What if we need more than the plan includes?"**
"You can upgrade anytime, or just pay for overages at $0.01 per query. We also offer annual plans with a 15% discount."

## Demo Tips & Best Practices

### Do's
✅ Customize questions to their industry and role
✅ Let them ask questions during the demo
✅ Show real documents (sanitized if needed)
✅ Be honest about limitations
✅ Focus on their specific pain points
✅ Keep energy high and pace brisk

### Don'ts
❌ Rush through the demo
❌ Use generic examples that don't relate to them
❌ Oversell or make unrealistic promises
❌ Skip the problem validation
❌ Forget to schedule next steps
❌ Talk more than 60% of the time

### Handling Technical Issues
- Always have a backup demo environment
- Pre-record a demo video as fallback
- If something breaks, acknowledge it and move on
- "Let me show you a screenshot of that feature instead"

### Reading the Room
**Engaged signals:**
- Asking specific questions
- Taking notes
- Nodding along
- "How would this work for [specific scenario]?"

**Disengaged signals:**
- Multitasking (typing, looking away)
- Short answers
- Checking the time
- "Send me information and I'll review it"

**If disengaged:**
- Ask a direct question: "Is this still relevant to your needs?"
- Offer to reschedule: "Happy to continue this when you have more time"
- Get to the point: "Let me show you the one thing that matters most..."

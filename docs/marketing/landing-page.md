BaceVector turns your internal documents into an intelligent knowledge base. Upload your files, and let employees ask questions in plain English.

1. **Upload Your Documents** 📤
   Upload PDFs, Word docs, and text files. We support SOPs, handbooks, policies, technical specs, and more.

2. **AI Learns Your Content** 🧠
   Our AI reads and understands your documents, creating a searchable knowledge base unique to your company.

3. **Get Instant Answers** ⚡
   Employees ask questions naturally. BaceVector finds the exact information and cites sources.

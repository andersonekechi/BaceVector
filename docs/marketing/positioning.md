# BaceVector Positioning & Messaging

## Positioning Statement

**For** mid-size businesses (50-500 employees)

**Who** struggle with employees wasting time searching internal documents and asking repetitive questions,

**BaceVector** is an AI-powered internal knowledge base

**That** turns your documents into an intelligent Q&A system, delivering instant, accurate answers with source citations.

**Unlike** traditional search tools or generic AI chatbots,

**BaceVector** is purpose-built for internal company documents with enterprise security, usage tracking, and transparent pricing.

### vs. Traditional Search (SharePoint, Google Drive search)
- **Them:** Keyword matching, requires exact terms
- **Us:** Natural language understanding, contextual answers

### vs. Generic AI Chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude)
- **Them:** Generic knowledge, no company context, data privacy concerns

### vs. Enterprise Knowledge Management (Confluence, Notion)

- **Us:** Automatic understanding, direct Q&A interface

### vs. Custom AI Solutions
- **Them:** Expensive ($50k+), 6-month implementation, requires ML team
- **Us:** $49-$149/month, 30-minute setup, no technical expertise needed

## Objection Handling

### "We already have SharePoint/Confluence"
**Response:** "Those are great for organizing documents, but employees still need to know where to look. BaceVector works alongside your existing tools, making everything instantly searchable with natural language."

### "Can't we just use ChatGPT?"
**Response:** "ChatGPT doesn't know your company's specific information and raises data privacy concerns. BaceVector is trained exclusively on your documents with enterprise security and source citations."

### "This seems expensive"
**Response:** "Consider the cost of employees spending 2 hours daily searching for information. At $50/hour, that's $100/day per employee. BaceVector pays for itself if it saves just 30 minutes per week."

### "What if the AI gives wrong answers?"
**Response:** "Every answer includes source citations, so employees can verify the information. Plus, you control which documents are included and can update them anytime."

### "We're worried about data security"
**Response:** "We use enterprise-grade encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and never use your data to train AI models. Your documents stay in secure cloud storage with role-based access controls."

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
- **Professional but approachable** - We're serious about productivity, not stuffy
- **Clear and direct** - No jargon, no fluff
- **Confident but humble** - We know we're good, but we listen
- **Empowering** - You're the expert on your business, we're the tool

### Tone Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use "you" and "your" (customer-focused)
- Be specific with numbers and examples
- Acknowledge pain points directly
- Celebrate customer wins

**Don't:**
- Overhype with superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
- Use technical jargon without explanation
- Make promises we can't keep
- Dismiss competitor solutions

### Example Copy

**Good:**
"Upload your employee handbook and let your team ask questions naturally. 'What's our vacation policy?' gets an instant answer with the exact section cited."

**Bad:**
"Leverage our revolutionary AI-powered semantic search engine to unlock transformational knowledge management paradigms."

## Sales Enablement

### Discovery Questions

1. "How much time does your team spend searching for information daily?"
2. "What types of documents do employees need to reference most often?"
3. "How do new hires currently learn company policies and procedures?"
4. "What's your biggest frustration with your current knowledge management?"
5. "Who gets interrupted most with repetitive questions?"

### Demo Script (15 minutes)

**Minutes 1-3:** Problem validation
- "Show me your current process for finding [specific info]"
- Empathize with pain points

**Minutes 4-10:** Product walkthrough
- Upload sample document (their industry)
- Ask 3-4 natural language questions
- Show source citations
- Demonstrate analytics dashboard

**Minutes 11-13:** Pricing & implementation
- Walk through plans
- Estimate their usage
- Explain 14-day trial

**Minutes 14-15:** Next steps
- Answer questions
- Schedule trial kickoff
- Provide case study

Company Vacation Policy

All employees are entitled to paid vacation time based on their years of service:
- 0-2 years: 10 days per year
- 3-5 years: 15 days per year
- 6+ years: 20 days per year

Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your direct manager. Vacation time cannot be carried over to the next year unless approved by HR.

Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 5 paid sick days per year. Sick leave can be used for personal illness or to care for a family member. Unused sick days do not roll over.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work days must be scheduled in advance. All remote workers must have a dedicated workspace and reliable internet connection.

Health Insurance

The company provides health insurance coverage for all full-time employees. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date. The company covers 80% of the premium cost.

Performance Reviews

Annual performance reviews are conducted in December. Reviews include self-assessment, manager feedback, and goal setting for the upcoming year. Performance ratings range from "Exceeds Expectations" to "Needs Improvement".
